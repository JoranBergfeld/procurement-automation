# Agent guidance

## Decisions

- When making or changing a material project decision, record its context, rationale, consequences and status in `docs/decisions/` and update its index.
- For Wayfinder work, the issue remains the canonical decision record; link the supporting document from the issue and distinguish partial decisions from completed resolutions.
- Before working on an affected area, consult the relevant records in `docs/decisions/README.md`. Keep records safe for this public repository.

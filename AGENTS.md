# Agent guidance

## Assumptions

- Record material, non-obvious assumptions that affect scope, architecture, behavior, security or privacy, compliance, cost, or acceptance in `docs/assumptions/`.
- Create or update a topic record and the directory index when an assumption is introduced or materially changes. State its status, rationale and impact; separate evidence from inference; cite source decisions; and name a validation owner and trigger when known. Mark unknown owners or triggers explicitly.
- Keep assumptions distinct from verified facts and settled decisions. Never present an unverified assumption as a fact, approval, or compliance determination.
- Keep records safe for this public repository. Store no confidential source material, personal data, supplier bids, or procurement audit records here; use a sanitized reference to the authorized source instead.
- For a Wayfinder decision, its issue resolution remains canonical. Link supporting assumption records from the issue rather than duplicating the decision.
- When a Wayfinder map is active, review its settled resolutions for material assumptions that remain conditional or unverified. Use research findings as evidence, not as assumptions by default.

See `docs/assumptions/README.md` for the record format and index.

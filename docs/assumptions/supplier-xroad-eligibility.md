# Supplier X-Road eligibility

**Status:** Conditional; no specific supplier or service access confirmed.

## Assumption

Qualify suppliers individually before onboarding. Do not assume that general foreign-supplier eligibility establishes a specific supplier's membership, representative authority, e-seal credentials, hosted/shared Security Server arrangement, or access to the procurement service.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Research found that foreign organizations may join X-Road as NEE members subject to RIA requirements, and that hosted/shared Security Servers are permitted under stated conditions. This does not confirm a particular supplier's credentials, service authorization, or route.

## Impact and validation

Supplier onboarding and production exchange depend on evidence for the supplier's membership, representation, e-seal, Security Server arrangement, and service access. The confirming owner and any supplier-specific outcome are unknown.

# AI Act classification and logging

**Status:** Planning assumption; legal classification unassessed.

## Assumption

For planning, design safeguards and logging so the system can meet high-risk AI requirements if they apply. This is not a legal classification of the system, a finding that it is high-risk, or confirmation of compliance.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The research resolution says AI Act logging and retention duties depend on high-risk classification and intended purpose; it does not classify this procurement-agent use. No competent authority or legal-function confirmation was obtained for this map decision.

## Impact and validation

Treat classification-specific obligations as unresolved. Before production, the competent legal/compliance owner must assess intended purpose, classification, and resulting logging duties. The owner and assessment date are unknown.

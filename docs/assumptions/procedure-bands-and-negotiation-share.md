# Procedure bands and negotiation share

**Status:** Unverified planning assumption.

## Assumption

For simple procurements, approximately 20% is used as an illustrative share in which negotiation is reserved. The research resolution gives a plausible range of 10%-50%; only about 2%-4% is visible in notices, because reservation may be stated in procurement documents instead.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The 20% figure is not a measured national rate. It is a planning value within an uncertain range, not a confirmed share. The visible notice rate does not establish the rate across procurement documents.

## Impact and validation

The assumed share affects the estimated population eligible for negotiation. It must not be applied to all procedure types or call-offs, whose legal negotiation conditions differ.

Validate by sampling simple-procurement documents and recording whether negotiation was reserved. The owner is unknown.

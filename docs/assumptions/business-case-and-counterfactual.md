# Business case and counterfactual

These are planning premises from settled Wayfinder decisions, not observed programme results or a validated investment case.

## Assumptions

### Agent effects can be distinguished from reform effects

**Status:** Conditional; to be tested.

The value model assumes a credible post-reform, no-agent comparison can be constructed to attribute programme effects. Randomisation is preferred if lawful and feasible; matching is the stated fallback, but its comparability remains to be demonstrated.

**Impact if false:** Agent-attributable benefits cannot be estimated with the planned comparison.

**Validation:** Test feasibility in the controlled implementation. Owner unknown.

### Transferred effort benchmarks are useful for initial planning

**Status:** Externally transferred; not locally validated.

Buyer-effort references from other jurisdictions are used for initial planning scenarios despite not being measurements of Estonian procurement-agent outcomes.

**Impact if false:** Illustrative effort and capacity estimates may not represent Estonia.

**Validation:** Compare with local buyer-effort observations in the controlled implementation. Owner unknown.

### Productivity and adoption scenarios are useful planning cases

**Status:** Unverified scenario assumptions; not forecasts.

The model uses 20%, 35%, and 50% effort-reduction cases and three-, five-, and seven-year adoption paths with a linear ramp. These are unweighted illustrative scenarios, not measured outcomes, likelihood estimates, or low/base/high forecasts.

**Impact if false:** The illustrative capacity totals and adoption trajectories will not describe actual programme performance.

**Validation:** Compare effort by workflow and buyer context, and adoption, with observations from the controlled implementation. Owner unknown.

### Provisional appraisal parameters are acceptable

**Status:** Conditional; funder confirmation outstanding.

A five-year post-go-live appraisal horizon and 4% real discount rate are provisional parameters, not confirmed funder requirements.

**Impact if false:** NPV and payback assessments must be recalculated using the funder's required horizon and rate.

**Validation:** Confirm funder requirements before final appraisal. Owner unknown.

### Productivity and speed improvements are achievable

**Status:** Unverified planning thesis.

The planning thesis assumes the common agent-assisted system may reduce buyer effort and published-tender time to signature versus the post-reform no-agent case while meeting safeguards. The model is illustrative and does not establish that these effects will occur.

**Impact if false:** The principal productivity and time-to-market benefits would not be realised.

**Validation:** Controlled implementation with a comparable post-reform no-agent case. Owner unknown.

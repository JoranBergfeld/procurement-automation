# Procurement records and retention

**Status:** Planning assumption; authority-specific schedule unconfirmed.

## Assumption

Plan to preserve attributable material procurement interactions, decisions, signatures, and evidence under the applicable authority records schedule, with access controls. Do not retain all model prompts and completions by default. No single retention period is assumed for every record.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The research resolution found no general rule that every prompt or completion must be retained. It identified conditional minimums: at least six months for covered high-risk AI logs under the AI Act, subject to other applicable law; and at least three years after award for procurement documentation within Directive 2014/24/EU scope. Neither minimum establishes the authority's complete records schedule or applies universally.

## Impact and validation

Retention settings and access policy remain unapproved. Before production, the authority's legal/records owner must identify the applicable schedule, record classes, periods, and access rules. The owner and schedule are unknown.

# Business case model and evidence

## Status

This is the current index to the agent-to-agent public procurement business case. The agreed output is a planning thesis, illustrative model and KPI framework, not a validated national investment case. The national rollout decision follows a controlled implementation; no positive return has yet been established.

The [business case and KPI map](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/1) remains open while its 15 decision tickets are closed. The separate [post-implementation validation issue](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/14) is open and outside the map.

## Current decisions

- Compare agent-assisted procurement with **post-reform procurement without agents**. Do not attribute effects of the 1 November 2026 legal reform to the agent system. ([Evidence strategy](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/13); [2026 reform](https://www.fin.ee/uudised/riigihangete-susteem-muutub-sugisest-oluliselt-kiiremaks).)
- Model buyer productivity, publication-to-signature time for published tenders, and specifically evidenced public cost savings. Time freed is capacity, not cash; supplier effort and outcomes are reported separately. ([Value model](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/7); [planning model](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/9).)
- Track purchase prices, but do not forecast price reductions as benefits. Contract-price changes alone do not establish savings or net economic gain. The later planning decision supersedes the earlier price-forecast clause in the value-model resolution. ([Planning model](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/9); [KPI framework](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/8).)
- Keep the 20%, 35% and 50% effort assumptions and three-, five- and seven-year adoption paths as **unweighted planning scenarios**, not low/base/high or likely forecasts. Report outcomes by measured procurement workflow and buyer context before any weighted total. ([Planning model](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/9); [targets and validation](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/10).)
- Use elapsed hours for duration KPIs. Full-flow lead time requires an observed initiation event; publication-to-signature applies only when there is a publication. Show data coverage and exclusions rather than inventing timestamps or substituting metrics. ([KPI framework](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/8); [event model](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/issues/15).)

## Current model

[Download the productivity and speed prototype](business-case-productivity-prototype.xlsx). It is an assumption-driven partial illustration, not observed performance or a financial return. It uses 2025 procedure counts re-banded to 2026 rules and transferred historical effort references for only two procedure groups; below-simple-threshold purchases and framework/DPS call-offs are excluded. At full adoption, its 35% scenario yields 198,415 hours of illustrative capacity, not cash savings. Its duration figures are pre-reform references, not the post-reform no-agent counterfactual.

The [older evidence pack](https://github.com/JoranBergfeld/procurement-automation/blob/joranbergfeld-business-case-wayfinder-map/docs/business-case-evidence.md) is retained on its original branch for historical research context. It predates the value-model and planning decisions: its forecast price effects, "base" case and full-production claims are not the current model and must not be used as agreed benefits.

## Evidence and limits

- Estonia reported **9,137 published procurements nationwide in 2025**, with estimated aggregate value over EUR 8 billion. Estimated tender value is not the same as realised spend or programme-eligible spend. ([Ministry of Finance announcement](https://www.fin.ee/uudised/riigihangete-maht-uletas-2025-aastal-kaheksa-miljardi-euro-piiri); [2025 statistics](https://www.fin.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2026-05/2025.a%20riigihangete%20valdkonna%20statistika%20ja%20kokkuv%C3%B5te_.pdf).)
- The OECD reports procurement at **15.3% of Estonia's GDP in 2023**. Treat this as context, not the addressable budget or an agent-attributable benefit. ([OECD, Strategic Public Procurement and Professionalisation Initiatives in Estonia](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/strategic-public-procurement-and-professionalisation-initiatives-in-estonia_56e03f80-en/full-report.html).)
- The previously published case-study claims of a 60% cycle-time reduction and over EUR 30 million annual benefit are not established model results. The 2026 reform already changes thresholds and expected procedure duration, so comparison must use the post-reform baseline. ([Case study](case-study16.md); [reform announcement](https://www.fin.ee/uudised/riigihangete-susteem-muutub-sugisest-oluliselt-kiiremaks).)
- The Public Procurement Register already supports electronic submission and communication, automatic evaluation, and contract management. The no-agent baseline must reflect actual use of existing digital services. ([Register services](https://www.fin.ee/en/public-procurement-state-aid-and-assets/public-procurement-policy/public-procurement-register).)
- The European Commission reports that 94% of Estonia's recorded bids in 2024 were SME bids. This is a share of bids, not the KPI for the share of procurements with SME participation. Its comparable decision-speed indicator starts at bid deadline and ends at award; it excludes framework agreements and is not the project's full-flow measure. ([Estonia scoreboard](https://single-market-scoreboard.ec.europa.eu/countries/estonia_en); [indicator methodology](https://single-market-scoreboard.ec.europa.eu/index.php/business-framework-conditions/public-procurement_en).)

## Still to establish

Programme-specific costs, the controlled implementation budget, eligible-population coverage, post-reform outcome baselines, and a credible same-reform comparison remain to be established. The five-year NPV horizon and 4% real rate are provisional pending funder confirmation. Payback and NPV are not computable without the costs and evidenced cash flows. These are later decisions or implementation evidence, not inputs to claim as settled in this planning model.

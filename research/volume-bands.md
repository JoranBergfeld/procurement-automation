# Volume and spend by procedure band after the November 2026 reform

Ticket: #12 · Map: #1 · Compiled: Nov 2026 · Base year: 2025 (latest complete register year), re-banded under the thresholds in force from 1 Nov 2026

## Answer

**Re-banded under the post-reform thresholds, 2025 has about 9,100–9,200 registered procurements:**

| Share | Band | Procedures | Estimated value |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~33% | Below the new simple-procurement threshold | ~2,900 | ~€0.27bn |
| ~39% | New simple procurements, €50k/€100k up to the EU threshold | ~3,550 | ~€1.3bn |
| ~22% | At or above the EU threshold | ~2,000 | €3.6–6.7bn, by residual |
| ~8% | Grant-recipient purchases | ~730 | ~€0.37bn |

The first row includes ~1,230 procedures that the reform pushes out of mandatory registration (MoF: 1,222 worth €90M on 2024 data).

- **Weight of the lower bands:** the two lower bands are **~70% of procedures but only ~10–25% of value**. The money stays in the EU-threshold band and in frameworks. Frameworks are ~23% of procedures but **56% of value**.
- **Negotiation in simple procurements:** at least **~2–4%** of simple procurements say in the notice that they reserve negotiation (a lower bound from the notice text). The true share is plausibly **10–50%**, because the reservation usually sits in the procurement documents, which are not in the open data. *(A6)*
- **Framework call-offs:**
  - **~80% of frameworks are let without reopening of competition**, so their call-offs have no competitive step to automate.
  - Only **~126 mini-competitions** are run in the register each year (2024).
  - **~6,600 DPS call-off contracts** are concluded each year.
- **The "25,000 procedures" figure** in the case study is not a procedure count. It matches the **number of contracts and call-off transactions**:

  | Component | Count |
  |---|---|
  | Contracts | 14,850 |
  | DPS contracts | ~6,600 |
  | Grant-recipient purchases | ~724 |
  | Mini-competitions and other recorded call-offs | ~2–3k |
  | **Total** | **≈ 24–25k** |

  Use **9,137 procedures** and **14,850 contracts** (MoF 2025) as the authoritative counts.
- **Authoritative value:**
  - **Register value:** use **MoF Fig. 2 = €5,112M for 2025**. It is a first-vintage floor; past vintages were revised up by 6–37%, so expect about €5.4–7.0bn. The report text's "üle 8 miljardi" ("over €8bn") does not match its own figure. It most likely reflects the revised 2024 value (€8.33bn).
  - **Annual spend:** use **OECD general-government procurement, ~€6.2bn (2025)**. Register value is not spend: it includes multi-year framework ceilings and state-owned companies.
- **Unpublished below-threshold spend:** there is **no open invoice-level dataset**.
  - Best proxy: **€0.3–0.9bn per year** (5–15% of general-government procurement spend), cross-checked against State Treasury e-invoice and trial-balance data.
  - Settling it needs a formal **data request to RTK and the MoF**. The exact wording is in the "Data request" section below.

## Thresholds used (post-reform, from 1 Nov 2026)

| Contract type | Simple-procurement threshold (new) | Old simple / national threshold | EU threshold 2026–27 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic: goods and services | **€50k** | €30k / €60k | €140k (central government) / €216k (other buyers) |
| Classic: works | **€100k** | €60k / €150k | €5.404M |
| Utilities (network sectors) and defence: goods and services | **€100k** | €60k | ~€432k *(A1)* |
| Utilities and defence: works | **€500k** | €300k | €5.404M |
| Social and other specific services | **€500k** | €300k | €750k |
| Special services and design contests | **€100k** | €60k | per the EU thresholds |

Further rules:
- The national threshold is abolished, so everything between the simple-procurement threshold and the EU threshold becomes a simple procurement (lihthange).
- Below the simple-procurement threshold, registration is no longer mandatory (small purchases).

Sources: RT I, 03.07.2026, 3 (https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/103072026003); Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2152 (EU thresholds).

| **Contracts plus transactions** | **≈ 24–25k** | – |

**Conclusion:** the case study's 25k counts transactions or contracts, not procedures. Model the cost per procedure (€16.5k above the threshold; see the procedure-cost research) on **~9.1k procedures**. Use ~25k only for per-transaction workloads such as contract management and invoice matching.

Under the post-reform rules, the registered procedure count falls to **~6,000–8,000** (the high end applies if buyers keep registering voluntarily). This is because ~1,230 procedures (and possibly the ~1,700 voluntary ones) drop out of the register.

| Year | 2024 report | 2025 report | Revision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 4,167 | 4,172 | +0.1% |
| 2022 | 7,911 | 7,954 | +0.5% |
| 2023 | 6,504 | 6,909 | +6% |

The revision is front-loaded in the first year. I therefore expect the 2025 value to settle at **€5.4bn (low), €6.1bn (base) or €7.0bn (high)** *(A2)*.

Register value is not spend:
- It includes framework ceilings that run for up to 4 years.
- It includes state-owned companies and utilities (`pub-undert`), which hold ~€0.74bn of the valued amount.
- It excludes unregistered small purchases.

## Best proxy for unpublished below-threshold spend, and the data request

**No open dataset gives invoice-level or contract-level spend below the threshold.** I searched andmed.eesti.ee for "ostuarve" and "saldoandmik". The register's open data covers only notices.

Proxies, in order of usefulness:

1. **RTK general-government trial balances (saldoandmikud).** These give annual totals for operating costs (account group 55) and fixed-asset purchases (account group 15) per entity. Subtracting registered contract value gives an upper bound on unregistered purchases. They contain totals only, with no contract sizes.
2. **State Treasury / RTK e-invoice data (e-arved), per supplier, buyer and year.** Aggregating invoices per supplier and buyer pair, and counting pairs below €50k/€100k a year that have no register contract, gives the best estimate of small-purchase spend. This data is not public.
3. **Fallback:** 5–15% of ~€6.2bn = **€0.3–0.9bn**, base €0.6bn *(A3; unsourced judgement carried over from the procurement-baseline research)*.

**Exact data request.** Send it to RTK (register@riigihanked.riik.ee; the RTK analyst contact is Üllar Laan, ullar.laan@rtk.ee) and to the MoF public procurement policy department (rahandusministeerium, riigihangete poliitika osakond):

> Please provide, for procurements started in 2025 (and 2024 for comparison):
> 1. The PowerBI table behind Table 7 of the 788 SE explanatory memo, re-run on 2025 data. Count, estimated value and final contract value by post-reform band (below €50k/€100k simple threshold; simple procurement; ≥ EU threshold), split by sector (classic / utilities / defence / social) and contract nature.
> 2. The number and value of framework call-offs (orders) and mini-competitions recorded in the register in 2025, split by framework type (with or without reopening of competition) and by DPS.
> 3. From the State Treasury e-invoice / ledger data (RTK finance-accounting service): for general-government buyers, the number of supplier-buyer pairs and the total invoiced amount (excluding VAT) for 2025 by annual-aggregate band (< €10k, €10–50k, €50–100k, > €100k), flagged by whether a register contract exists.
> 4. The number of 2025 simple procurements whose procurement documents reserve the right to negotiate (a document text search, or a structured field if one exists).

## Assumptions (flagged)

- **A1.** The utilities and defence EU threshold for goods and services is taken as ~€432k for 2026–27. Reg. 2025/2152 values were checked only for the classic sector. This affects only a handful of procedures at the band 2/3 boundary.
- **A2.** The 2025 register value will be revised up by +6% (low) to +37% (high), base ~+20%. This is based on the 2023 and 2024 revisions only. Band 3 is computed as the residual.
- **A3.** Unregistered small purchases are 5–15% of general-government procurement spend. This is judgement, with no Estonian source.
- **A4.** Framework and DPS value is drawn down evenly over 3–4 years. The legal maximum is 4 years; many frameworks run for 2–3.
- **A5.** The residual ~2–2.5k transactions in the 25k reconciliation are recorded call-offs and market studies. The case study does not show its derivation.
- **A6.** The 2–4% of notices that mention negotiation is a lower bound, because the right to negotiate is usually reserved in the documents. Base 20% and high 50% are assumptions, supported only by the memo's remark that many buyers model simple-procurement rules on "an open procedure with the possibility of negotiation".
- **A7.** Imputation: 4,820 of 8,499 regular 2025 notices publish no value. They were assigned to bands using the band shares and mean values of valued notices with the same procedure code, sector subtype and nature. Missing values are probably not random: small procedures omit values more often. If so, band 1 is under-counted and band 3 over-valued.
- **A8.** The 2025 band thresholds are applied to 2025 estimated values in nominal terms. No behavioural response is modelled, such as buyers splitting or merging lots around the new thresholds.

## Method

1. Downloaded the 12 monthly eForms XML files of register open data for 2025 from `https://riigihanked.riik.ee/rhr/api/public/v1/opendata/notice/2025/month/{1..12}/xml`, ~40 MB each.
2. Parsed each notice: procedure code, eForms subtype, buyer legal type, contract nature, estimated value (lots summed), framework type and negotiation text. Kept the latest notice per procurement folder: 9,228 folders.
   - Grant recipients (`oth-single` with no buyer type): 729.
   - Procedure codes: `oth-single` 4,834, `open` 4,236, `restricted` 95, negotiated with call (`neg-w-call`) 45, `oth-mult` 13, competitive dialogue (`comp-dial`) 5.
3. Assigned each valued notice to a post-reform band using the threshold table above, and imputed values for unvalued notices (A7).
4. Took the counts, revisions, framework and DPS figures from the MoF annual statistics reports for 2024 and 2025, and from the 788 SE explanatory memo.
5. Found negotiation reservations by text search ("läbirääki") over notice Description and Note fields. A sample of matches confirms they are genuine reservations, e.g. "Hankija jätab endale õiguse pidada lihthankemenetluses läbirääkimisi" ("The contracting authority reserves the right to negotiate in the simple procurement").

## Open uncertainties

1. **Call-off volume and value without reopening.** The register's open data does not record orders. Only RTK can provide them (data request item 2).
2. **Below-threshold spend.** It is €0.3–0.9bn on an unsourced assumption. It needs the e-invoice aggregation (item 3).
3. **Share of simple procurements that reserve negotiation.** It lies between 2% (observed) and maybe 50%. A document-level text search is needed (item 4). This drives how much of the automation's negotiation module is legally usable.
4. **2025 value vintage.** The €5.1bn will be revised. Re-check the 2026 MoF report (expected ~May 2027) before finalising the business case.
5. **Voluntary registration after the reform.** If the ~1,700 voluntarily registered sub-€30k procedures and the 1,230 that move below the threshold stop being registered, the observable volume shrinks by ~30%. This weakens the data feed for any register-based automation.
6. **Contradictory "over €8bn" text in the MoF 2025 report.** It is unresolved. Ask the MoF to confirm which figure applies.

# Public Procurement Business Case

The terms used to assess the value of agent-to-agent public procurement.

## Language

**No-agent counterfactual**:
The procurement system and outcomes expected without the agent-to-agent programme, against which programme costs and benefits are assessed.

**Freed officer capacity**:
Procurement-officer time made available when procurement work requires less human effort. It does not by itself reduce government spending.

**Realised cash saving**:
Government spending demonstrably reduced or avoided relative to the no-agent counterfactual.

**Contract-price saving**:
A reduction in what a public buyer pays relative to the no-agent counterfactual, adjusted for quantity and quality. It can be a transfer from suppliers rather than an economy-wide gain.

**Government financial view**:
A perspective on procurement programme costs and benefits that accrue to government bodies. It excludes benefits that accrue only to suppliers.

**Economy-wide view**:
A perspective on procurement programme costs and benefits across affected parties, including government and suppliers.
